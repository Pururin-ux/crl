## 1. solve(ErdosProblems/184): mark `erdos_184.variants.covering` solved (Pyber 1985)

**Patch:** [`../patches/184_solved.lean`](../patches/184_solved.lean) — compiles.

The covering variant ("$n-1$ cycles and edges suffice if edge-disjointness is not
required", [Er71]) is exactly the Erdős–Gallai covering conjecture, proved by
L. Pyber, *An Erdős–Gallai conjecture*, *Combinatorica* **5** (1985), 67–79: every graph
on $n$ vertices can be covered by $n-1$ circuits and edges. The formal statement matches
(covering not decomposition; `Nonempty V`; `≤ n−1` cast to ℝ; a `Finset` of subgraphs —
deduplication only lowers the count; `n = 1` is witnessed by `D = ∅`).
Change: `answer(sorry)` → `answer(True)`, category → `research solved`, add `[Py85]`.

## 2. solve(ErdosProblems/1106): mark `erdos_1106.parts.i` solved (Schinzel; Schinzel–Wirsing)

**Patch:** [`../patches/1106_solved.lean`](../patches/1106_solved.lean) — compiles.

$F(n) \to \infty$ is known, and recorded on
[erdosproblems.com/1106](https://www.erdosproblems.com/1106) itself: Schinzel observed it
follows from the asymptotic for $p(n)$ plus a result of Tijdeman (details in Erdős–Iviç),
and Schinzel–Wirsing proved $F(n) \gg \log n$ (*Multiplicative properties of the partition
function*, Proc. Indian Acad. Sci. Math. Sci. **97** (1987)). `parts.ii` ($F(n) > n$)
remains open and is untouched. Also fixes the module docstring's `1064` link-text typo.
Change: `answer(sorry)` → `answer(True)`, category → `research solved`, add `[ScWi87]`.
